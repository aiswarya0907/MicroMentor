Problem & Solution 1 · The problem

Students are guessing at their futures with incomplete data.

Most undergrads research careers through family opinions, social media, and recruiter pitches — rarely people who've actually done the job.

Evidence status This prototype uses published desk research. The student quotations and alumni profiles are illustrative composites; UAE student interviews and a pilot are the next validation stage.

Who this hits hardest: upperclassmen choosing a first job, career-switchers, and first-generation students without insider networks to casually explain what roles actually involve or if AED 7,000/mo is livable in Dubai versus Sharjah.

Alumni want to help. But formal coffee chats and LinkedIn messages are high-stakes, hard to schedule, and awkward to navigate.

3 · The solution

Three low-commitment ways to get the real story.

The smaller the ask, the more honest the answer.

01 / Voice drop-ins

15-minute voice calls, or recorded answers on demand.

Request a quick voice drop-in with an alum. If no one is free live, send an async voice question and get a personalized recording back within days.

  • No formal interview format — simple 1:1 conversation
  • Auto-generated transcript provided for every clip
  • Ask sensitive compensation questions anonymously
